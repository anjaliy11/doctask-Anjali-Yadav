AMENDMENT NO. 1

Reference Contract: LICENSE AND HOSTING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: June 15, 2000

Parties: Corio Inc. ("CORIO") and Changepoint, Inc. ("CHANGEPOINT")


AMENDED CLAUSES:

1. Section 1.6 (Definition of Customer(s))
Original Language:
`"CUSTOMER(S)" means one or more customers of the Corio Services having its principal executive offices in North America who obtains a sublicense from Corio to use the Software by accessing one or more Corio Servers.`

New Language:
`"CUSTOMER(S)" means one or more customers of the Corio Services having its principal executive offices located anywhere worldwide who obtains a sublicense from Corio to use the Software by accessing one or more Corio Servers.`

2. Section 4.2 (Payment Terms)
Original Language:
`Corio shall pay all undisputed fees and invoices within thirty (30) days from the date of receipt of the applicable invoice.`

New Language:
`Corio shall pay all undisputed fees and invoices within sixty (60) days from the date of receipt of the applicable invoice.`

3. Section 8.2 (Termination for Convenience)
Original Language:
`Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause at any time by providing at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the non-terminating party.`

New Language:
`Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause at any time by providing at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the non-terminating party.`


CONTRADICTIONS INTRODUCED:

- Customer geographic scope changed from principal executive offices in North America to worldwide.
- Payment terms changed from Net-30 to Net-60.
- Termination notice period changed from 90 days to 30 days.

This is important for testing automated conflict detection.


All other terms and conditions of the Agreement remain unchanged.
