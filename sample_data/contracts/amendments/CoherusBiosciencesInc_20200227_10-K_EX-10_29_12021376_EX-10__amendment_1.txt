AMENDMENT NO. 1

Reference Contract: License and Development Agreement (Exhibit 10.29)

Effective Date: August 1, 2020

Parties: Bioeq IP AG ("Bioeq") and Coherus BioSciences, Inc. ("Licensee")


AMENDED CLAUSES:

1. Section 7.5 (Payment Terms)
Original Language: "All invoices and milestone or royalty payments due under this Agreement shall be payable within thirty (30) days from the date of receipt of the applicable invoice or payment trigger event."
New Language: "All invoices and milestone or royalty payments due under this Agreement shall be payable within sixty (60) days from the date of receipt of the applicable invoice or payment trigger event."

2. Section 15.2 (Termination for Breach)
Original Language: "Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the other Party in the event of a material breach by such other Party, provided such breach remains uncured during such ninety (90) day period."
New Language: "Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party in the event of a material breach by such other Party, provided such breach remains uncured during such thirty (30) day period."

3. Section 16.6 (Governing Law)
Original Language: "This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice of law principles."
New Language: "This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice of law principles."


CONTRADICTIONS INTRODUCED:

- Payment terms changed from Net-30 to Net-60.
- Termination notice period for material breach changed from 90 days to 30 days.
- Governing law changed from the State of New York to the State of Delaware.


All other terms and conditions of the Agreement remain unchanged.
