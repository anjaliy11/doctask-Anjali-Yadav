AMENDMENT NO. 1

Reference Contract: License and Hosting Agreement (Exhibit 10.5)

Effective Date: May 1, 2000

Parties: Corio Inc. ("CORIO") and Commerce One, Inc. ("COMMERCE ONE")


AMENDED CLAUSES:

1. Section 1.1 ("SOFTWARE USER")
   - Original Language: "The identification number and password used by a Software User is reusable and reassignable and may be used and transferred by Corio, in accordance with the licenses granted below, between Customers as one Customer discontinues the Corio Services and another Customer subscribes."
   - New Language: "The identification number and password used by a Software User is non-reusable and non-transferable. Under no circumstances may an identification number or password be reassigned or transferred by Corio to another Customer when a Customer discontinues Corio Services."

2. Section 1.3 ("CORIO MARKET SEGMENT")
   - Original Language: '"CORIO MARKET SEGMENT" means those customers with annual sales revenues of less than $1,000,000,000.'
   - New Language: '"CORIO MARKET SEGMENT" means those customers with annual sales revenues of less than $250,000,000.'

3. Section 1.4 ("CORIO SERVERS")
   - Original Language: '"CORIO SERVERS" means the unlimited number of computer servers owned or operated by or for Corio which will contain the installed Software (as defined below) for access by Customers in connection with the Corio Services.'
   - New Language: '"CORIO SERVERS" means a maximum limit of twenty-five (25) computer servers owned or operated by or for Corio which will contain the installed Software for access by Customers in connection with the Corio Services.'


CONTRADICTIONS INTRODUCED:

- Software User credentials changed from reusable and reassignable between Customers to non-reusable and strictly non-transferable.
- Corio Market Segment customer qualification cap reduced from annual revenues of less than $1,000,000,000 to less than $250,000,000.
- Server infrastructure allowance changed from an unlimited number of computer servers to a capped limit of 25 computer servers.


All other terms and conditions of the Agreement remain unchanged.
