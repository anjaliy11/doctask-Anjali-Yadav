AMENDMENT NO. 1

Reference Contract: DISTRIBUTOR AGREEMENT (Exhibit 10.5)

Effective Date: October 21, 2005

Parties: VendingData Corporation, a Nevada corporation ("Vendor"), and Technical Casino Supplies Ltd, an English company ("Distributor").


AMENDED CLAUSES:

1. Section 1 (TERM)
Original Language:
"As provided for in this Section 1, the term of this Agreement shall be for a period of five (5) years, beginning on the Effective Date (the "Initial Term"); provided, however, the Initial Term shall be subject to automatic successive renewal terms of three (3) years each (the "Renewal Terms" and together with the Initial Term, the "Term")."

New Language:
"As provided for in this Section 1, the term of this Agreement shall be for a period of five (5) years, beginning on the Effective Date (the "Initial Term"); provided, however, any renewal of the Initial Term or subsequent terms shall require the mutual written consent of both parties delivered at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the relevant Term, and shall not renew automatically."

2. Section 1.1.1 (Termination Notice)
Original Language:
"1.1.1. By either Vendor or Distributor, upon written notice of termination of this Agreement no later than ninety (90) calendar days prior to the expiration of the relevant Term, then in effect;"

New Language:
"1.1.1. By either Vendor or Distributor, upon written notice of termination of this Agreement no later than thirty (30) calendar days prior to the expiration of the relevant Term, then in effect;"

3. Section 1.1.3 (Termination for Material Breach)
Original Language:
"1.1.3. By either Vendor or Distributor, following a material or continuing breach of this Agreement (in the case of a breach which is capable of remedy) by the other party and the breaching party's failure to cure such breach within ninety (90) days of receiving written notice of such breach..."

New Language:
"1.1.3. By either Vendor or Distributor, following a material or continuing breach of this Agreement (in the case of a breach which is capable of remedy) by the other party and the breaching party's failure to cure such breach within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice of such breach..."


CONTRADICTIONS INTRODUCED:

- Renewal structure changed from automatic successive 3-year renewal terms to requiring explicit written consent prior to expiration.
- Non-renewal/termination notice prior to term expiration changed from ninety (90) calendar days to thirty (30) calendar days.
- Cure period for material breach changed from ninety (90) days to thirty (30) days.


All other terms and conditions of the Agreement remain unchanged.
